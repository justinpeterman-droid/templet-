# Research — the Project's Content & IA Brief

**This is the project brief.** These documents define *what* we're building — a
therapeutic **hypnotherapy practice website** (Hometown Serenity): its
information architecture, conversion funnel, digital-audio e-commerce,
trust/credibility strategy, SEO, and the US legal/compliance requirements for a
health practice.

It is the authoritative content/IA source-of-truth. The engineering *methodology*
for building it lives in `docs/phases/` + `docs/00_MASTER_PROJECT.md`; this
research is what those phases implement (it feeds Phase 2 onward).

## Contents

| File | What it is |
|------|------------|
| `Hometown_Serenity_Sitemap_and_Conversion_Blueprint.md` | Full sitemap, conversion-funnel design, digital-product e-commerce, trust/SEO, and legal/compliance blueprint for a therapeutic hypnotherapy practice (2026). |
| `Hypnotherapy_Website_Design_Research.pdf` | Source research document (8 pages) the blueprint draws on. |

## How it maps to the build phases

- **Phase 2 (UX Architecture):** the blueprint's sitemap and "Project/content"
  fields feed the content model and `SITEMAP.md`.
- **Phase 3 (Shell):** nav structure (~5–7 items) and the five→many route set.
- **Phase 8 (Contact Form):** the discovery-call / lead-magnet capture flow and
  its consent requirements.
- **Phase 10 (SEO):** problem-first landing pages and E-E-A-T/YMYL guidance.
- **Footer/legal:** the required utility pages (Privacy, Consumer Health Data
  Privacy, Disclaimer, Scope of Practice, Accessibility, Cookies).

Nothing here is legal advice; see the blueprint's own Caveats section.
